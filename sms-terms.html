Text message terms

Last updated November 12, 2026

Program

SYPP Cloud Bar Event Updates is run by SYPP Cloud Bar ("SYPP", "we"). It sends text messages to retail partner contacts about in-store events they have booked with us: booking confirmations, reminders, scheduling changes, day-of arrival and setup logistics, and replies to questions you text us. We don't send advertising or promotional offers by text.

How you sign up

  • Online: by checking the optional text message box on our booking form and giving your mobile number.
  • By phone: by telling our events coordinator on a call that you'd like text updates. The coordinator reads you these terms in short form (what we text, how often, that rates may apply, and that you can reply STOP or HELP), and we record the date and time of your consent.

Our first text confirms your sign-up and tells you how to opt out. Signing up is never a condition of booking an event.

How often

Message frequency varies with your bookings, usually 2 to 6 messages per event.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile plan. We don't charge for text messages.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You'll get one final message confirming you've opted out, and we won't text you again unless you sign up again (reply START). Reply HELP for help, or email info@syppit.com. Our texts come from our events line (shown on every text we send).

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers include the major US carriers. Delivery isn't guaranteed.

Your information

We use your mobile number only to run this program. We don't share or sell mobile numbers or text message consent to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
