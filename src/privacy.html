This policy explains how {{OPERATOR}} ("SYPP", "we", "us") handles information we collect through events.syppit.com, by email, by phone and by text message when we arrange in-store events with retail partners.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, role, store name and address, email, phone and mobile number.
  • Booking details: event dates and times, promotions, notes and logos you send.
  • Communications: emails, text messages and call recordings or notes between you and our team. We may record calls with our events coordinator for quality and record-keeping, and we tell you when a call is recorded.
  • Consent records: if you sign up for text messages, the date, time, method and wording of your consent.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends when you use our forms, such as IP address and browser type.

How we use it

  • To book, confirm, staff and support your events, and to send recaps afterward.
  • To reply to you by email, phone or text.
  • To send text messages you've agreed to under our Text Message Terms.
  • To keep business records, and to meet legal obligations.

Text messaging and your mobile number

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from every category of sharing described in this policy and will not be shared with any third parties. We use your mobile number only to send the event messages you signed up for and to reply to you. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

When we share information

We don't sell personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that run our systems for us (for example email, phone and text message delivery, cloud hosting, and the event staffing partners who send our Cloud Tenders, who receive only the store address, event time and the store's main phone number), under agreements that limit their use to providing that service.
  • With the brand whose event you booked, limited to the booking details needed to run and report on that event. Mobile numbers and text message consent are never included.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our partners or the public.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records and communications for as long as we work together and for up to three years after, unless the law requires longer. Call recordings are kept for up to 90 days. Records of text message consent and opt-outs are kept for at least four years.

Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, and we'll tell you if a breach affects your information as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing {{EMAIL}}. To stop text messages, reply STOP.

Children

Our services are for businesses and their staff. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
