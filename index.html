In-store Cloud Bar events for our retail partners

SYPP Cloud Bar runs staffed in-store brand events at retail partner locations across New York. We book the date with you, send a trained Cloud Tender with the full setup, and handle the logistics before and after.

How booking works

  1. You request a date and time, by phone with our events coordinator or with the booking form.
  2. We confirm staffing and send you a written confirmation with the date, time, promotion and on-site details.
  3. We send a reminder before the event and coordinate arrival and setup on the day.
  4. After the event you get a short recap.
Prefer text updates? When you book, you can choose to get confirmations, reminders and day-of logistics by text message. It's optional and never required to book. See our text message terms.
